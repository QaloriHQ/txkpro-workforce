DISPLAY-LABEL RULES
Internal enum values remain stable even when display language changes.
Example:
verified → UI label "Instructor Verified"
needs_practice → UI label "Needs More Practice"

RULES
- Never use free-text statuses as canonical workflow state.
- Add new states through reviewed schema/product changes.
- UI labels may be friendlier than internal values.
- Historical states must not be silently rewritten when a new state is introduced.
- Every state transition with authorization significance must be auditable.

END

D-10 APPROVED CLARIFICATION — 2026-10-03
Official placement requires an authorized Employer or permitted workforce operator confirming the Student started employment. Scheduled starts remain pending_start; retention milestones remain separate. Recording a hire alone does not establish started employment. Historical and scoped official counts require explicit confirmation evidence. Existing active/ended status values and PLACEMENT_CREATED / PLACEMENT_STATUS_CHANGED audit vocabulary remain; no retention status is a placement status. Role, evidence, reporting, legacy-history and verification details: docs/D10_OFFICIAL_PLACEMENT.md (#202).

W12-16A OWNER-APPROVED EXPANSION — 2026-10-04 (#223)
Portfolio project visibility: private/public; file access: private/public/employer. These are sharing controls, not Student discoverability or credential lifecycle statuses. Public files additionally require an active published public profile. Employer file access requires canonical approved talent scope and Student consent. New audit action: STUDENT_PORTFOLIO_CHANGED. No competency/hiring lifecycle changes. Contract: docs/W12_16A_STUDENT_PORTFOLIO.md.

KYU/AuthCard sandbox (owner approved 2026-10-07, #257): separate KYU identity, KYU payment, AuthCard share and immutable per-order consent contracts are defined in docs/governance/AUTHCARD_STAGING.md. These never change competency, hiring, placement or retention states.

APPENDIX C — OWNER APPROVED 2026-10-07
EMPLOYER_SCREENING_POLICY effective states: required, accepted, superseded, revoked. Derived from immutable policy versions, append-only acceptance and revocation evidence; historical records never rewritten.

APPENDIX D — COMM-02 OWNER APPROVED 2026-10-12 (#266)
COMMUNITY_POST_STATUS: draft, published, hidden, removed (existing canonical profile-post states; no competing post store).
COMMUNITY_POST_VISIBILITY: public, private, unlisted. COMM-02 creates public posts only; group audience is deferred.
COMMUNITY_REPORT_STATUS: submitted, reviewing, resolved, dismissed.
Community preferences, follows and saves are not workflow statuses. Likes/follows/saves do not create PRO Point ledger entries in COMM-02.

APPENDIX E — W12-20 OWNER APPROVED 2026-10-12 (#59)
PROFILE_POST_TYPE: post, article, update.
PROFILE_COMMENT_STATUS: published, hidden, removed.
PROFILE_INTERACTION_TYPE: like, comment, share, repost. A withdrawn like/share/repost removes the current canonical interaction record while immutable audit evidence remains. Hiding public activity history changes presentation only and never deletes canonical interaction records.
Only published public posts from active Workforce authors are interactable. Interaction records do not expose private Workforce events and do not award PRO Points in W12-20.

APPENDIX F — W12-18 / W12-19 / W12-21 OWNER APPROVED 2026-10-13 (#57, #58, #60)
ORGANIZATION_PUBLICATION_VISIBILITY: public, private. The canonical wf_public_pages publication status remains draft, published, unpublished; organization editors derive published from public and unpublished from private.
PROFILE_REVIEW_STATUS: pending, published, hidden, removed. New and revised reviews enter pending. Only published reviews appear publicly or contribute to rating summaries. Hidden and removed records remain auditable; removed reviews cannot be restored by the reviewer.
PROFILE_REVIEW_REPORT_STATUS: open, reviewing, resolved, dismissed. Report state is private moderation data and never appears in the public review projection.

APPENDIX G — W12-16B OWNER APPROVED 2026-10-13 (#224)
PRO_POINTS_SEASON_STATUS: open, finalized. Open is the derived state before an ended calendar quarter is finalized; finalized is represented by the immutable canonical finalization record. A finalized season never reopens. Later ledger corrections are append-only evidence and do not rewrite the season status or standings.
PRO_POINTS_LEVEL_VERSION: scheduled, effective. These are date-derived display states, not mutable database statuses. A version is scheduled before its future quarter and effective from that quarter until the next version. Threshold versions are immutable and do not retroactively change finalized seasons.

APPENDIX H — COMM-04 OWNER APPROVED 2026-10-13 (#268)
COMMUNITY_REPORT_STATUS remains: submitted, reviewing, resolved, dismissed. Automation state, severity and outcome are separate fields and never replace the canonical report lifecycle.
COMMUNITY_MODERATION_SEVERITY: low, medium, high, critical.
COMMUNITY_MODERATION_AUTOMATION_STATE: pending, monitoring, actioned, needs_review, failed, overridden.
COMMUNITY_MODERATION_APPEAL_STATUS: submitted, reviewing, upheld, reversed.
COMMUNITY_RESTRICTION_STATUS: active, expired, revoked. Expiration is time-derived and an expired restriction grants no authority to deny access.
COMMUNITY_BLOCK_MODE: mute, block. Mute removes the selected member's content from the viewer's signed-in feed. Block also denies direct community interaction in both directions and clears follows; it never deletes canonical content or audit history.

APPENDIX I — COMM-05 OWNER APPROVED 2026-10-14 (#269)
MESSAGE_REQUEST_STATUS: pending, accepted, declined, blocked, expired. Pending requests expire after seven days. Declined requests cannot be retried by the same sender for 30 days; block prevents retry until removed.
CONVERSATION_STATUS: active, archived, closed. COMM-05 direct conversations contain exactly two active natural-person Workforce members.
MESSAGE_STATUS: sent, redacted, removed. Messages are immutable in V1: a sender may redact their message to a tombstone, and moderation may remove or restore specifically reported content while retaining restricted evidence.
Conversation membership, mute and read timestamps are access/presentation state, not message lifecycle statuses. V1 has no automatic content purge; account deletion and legal/moderation evidence follow the approved tombstone and hold policy.
COMMUNITY EVENT (COMM-09)
draft | published | cancelled | completed
RSVP: interested | going | not_going

PRO ENGAGEMENT AWARD (COMM-10)
active | reversed
Active is represented by wf_pro_engagement_awards.active=true. Reversed keeps the same immutable logical award identity with active=false. A later valid restoration reactivates that identity; it does not create another award. These states do not reopen finalized seasons, and student ranking eligibility remains separate from points/level eligibility.

APPENDIX J — ADS-01 OWNER APPROVED 2026-10-16 (#265, #275)
ADVERTISER_STATUS: pending_review, active, rejected, suspended.
AD_CAMPAIGN_STATUS: draft, submitted, in_review, approved, scheduled, active, paused, completed, rejected, cancelled.
AD_CREATIVE_STATUS: draft, submitted, approved, rejected, archived.
AD_DELIVERY_EVENT_TYPE: served, viewable, clicked, invalidated, provider_reconciled. Asset load is not viewability. Delivery evidence is append-only; corrections use invalidated or provider_reconciled rather than rewriting prior events.
Only an active advertiser, active in-window campaign, approved creative, enabled placement and non-revoked manual funding authorization are eligible for ADS-01 delivery. Sponsored engagement never creates PRO Points.

APPENDIX K — ADS-02 OWNER APPROVED 2026-10-16 (#264, #276)
ADVERTISER_MEMBERSHIP_ROLE: advertiser_owner, advertiser_admin, advertiser_creative, advertiser_analyst.
ADVERTISER_MEMBERSHIP_STATUS: invited, active, suspended, revoked.
Open advertiser registration creates a pending_review advertiser and an active advertiser_owner membership. Advertiser approval never creates a Workforce app_role_membership. Campaign activation additionally requires an approved creative, valid schedule and placements, and a non-revoked manual allocation; ADS-02 performs no live charging.
