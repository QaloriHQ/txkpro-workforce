TXKPRO™ WORKFORCE PLATFORM
MVP1 PRODUCT REQUIREMENTS DOCUMENT (PRD)

Version: 1.0
Date: September 2026
Primary region: Greater Texarkana
Primary users: Students, Educators/Instructors, Employers/Contractors, Institution Admins, TXKPRO Admins

1. PURPOSE

TXKPRO Workforce MVP1 is a secure, local, three-sided workforce ecosystem connecting technical students, instructors/institutions, and vetted skilled-trade employers.

The MVP replaces fragmented placement workflows, unverified résumés, and manual referral processes with:
- a living Verified Skills Matrix;
- job-readiness and operational-fit signals;
- instructor referrals and employer discovery;
- interview and hire status tracking;
- 30/60/90-day retention monitoring;
- institution/employer reporting.

The supplied documents are the source of truth. Requirements labeled “MVP1 decision” are implementation decisions derived from those documents rather than direct source statements.

3. PRODUCT VISION

TXKPRO Workforce becomes the trusted local “lab-to-field” network for skilled trades.

Students prove what they can do without relying on a traditional résumé.
Educators verify competence and refer talent without manual phone/email placement work.
Employers identify local, job-ready, instructor-validated talent before students enter the open market.
Institutions and TXKPRO measure whether placements survive the first 90 days.

MVP1 is not a general ATS, social network, LMS, or national job board.

4. PRODUCT PRINCIPLES

P1. VERIFIED OVER SELF-REPORTED
Instructor-verified skills must be visibly and logically distinct from student self-attestation.

P2. LOCAL LIQUIDITY OVER NATIONAL SCALE
Optimize first for dense regional participation.

P3. LOW FRICTION
Core workflows should take a few clicks/taps and be mobile friendly.

P4. HUMAN HIRING DECISIONS
The platform may filter and organize candidates using explicit criteria, but MVP1 will not automatically accept, reject, or rank candidates for employers.

P5. DATA MINIMIZATION
Collect only workforce data needed to support placement and retention.

P6. AUDITABILITY
Skill verification, referrals, interview requests, hires, approvals, and retention interventions must be traceable.

P7. PILOTABILITY
Support a controlled 12-week single-cohort pilot before broad rollout.

7. PRIMARY END-TO-END WORKFLOW

1. TXKPRO approves an institution.
2. Institution creates/activates technical program and cohort.
3. Educators are scoped to institution/program/cohort.
4. Students are invited or roster-imported.
5. Students complete account and readiness profile.
6. Students self-attest applicable skills.
7. Instructors verify mastered curriculum/lab skills.
8. Employer is approved and completes hiring profile.
9. Employer browses/filters candidates.
10. Educator refers a student OR employer requests an interview.
11. Student responds.
12. Employer records a hire.
13. Hire date triggers Day 30/60/90 schedule.
14. Student receives pulse check-ins.
15. At-risk response creates intervention case.
16. Institution/TXKPRO dashboards update.

8. FUNCTIONAL REQUIREMENTS

8.1 AUTHENTICATION AND ONBOARDING

FR-AUTH-001 Support email/password authentication for Student, Educator, Employer.
FR-AUTH-002 Require email verification before workforce access.
FR-AUTH-003 Support forgot-password and reset-password.
FR-AUTH-004 Public signup never creates TXKPRO admin.
FR-AUTH-005 One stable identity may hold multiple server-controlled memberships.
FR-AUTH-006 Client role selection never grants authorization.
FR-AUTH-007 Role-specific onboarding for Student, Educator, Employer.
FR-AUTH-008 Educator access requires institution relationship approval.
FR-AUTH-009 Employer onboarding creates pending/verification state.
FR-AUTH-010 Track onboarding: not_started, in_progress, pending_review, complete.

Acceptance:
- Student can sign up, verify, sign in, onboard, sign out, recover password.
- Educator cannot access rosters by self-selecting educator.
- Unapproved employer cannot browse private talent.
- Admin cannot self-provision.

8.2 INSTITUTION / PROGRAM / COHORT / ROSTER

FR-INS-001 TXKPRO admin can create/approve institutions.
FR-INS-002 Institution admin can manage trade programs such as HVAC, Welding, Electrical, Mechatronics, or configured equivalents.
FR-INS-003 Institution admin can create cohorts with dates.
FR-INS-004 Institution admin can import student roster by CSV.
FR-INS-005 Import provides preview, validation, duplicate handling, confirmation.
FR-INS-006 Instructor access is scoped.
FR-INS-007 Institution sees onboarding and verification progress.

MVP1 decision:
Do not ingest grades, FAFSA/Pell, financial aid, or unrelated education data.

8.3 STUDENT WORKFORCE PROFILE

FR-STU-001 Profile includes:
- name;
- institution/program/cohort;
- graduation timing;
- location/service area;
- contact preferences;
- work availability;
- shift preferences;
- work-type preferences;
- employer discoverability.

FR-STU-002 Readiness fields include:
- valid driver’s license status;
- driving-record self-attestation;
- willingness to pass background screening;
- willingness to pass drug screening;
- preferred work environment/shift.

FR-STU-003 Readiness fields record source, timestamp, and verification status when applicable.
FR-STU-004 Student controls employer discoverability.
FR-STU-005 Self-attested data is labeled as such.

MVP1 decision:
Do not store raw MVR, drug-screen, or background-check reports.

8.4 VERIFIED SKILLS MATRIX

FR-SKL-001 Each program has a configurable skills matrix aligned to curriculum.
FR-SKL-002 Skill supports name, category/module, description, program, student state, educator verification state, verified_by, verified_at, optional evidence.
FR-SKL-003 Students may self-attest.
FR-SKL-004 Authorized instructors verify/revoke.
FR-SKL-005 Student edits never overwrite verification.
FR-SKL-006 Verification changes are audited.
FR-SKL-007 Employer view prioritizes verified skills.
FR-SKL-008 Institution can configure/import program matrix.

MVP1 decision:
No opaque aggregate employability score.

8.5 EDUCATOR REFERRAL

FR-REF-001 Authorized educator can refer a student to approved employer.
FR-REF-002 Referral stores student, educator, institution/program, employer, date, optional note, status.
FR-REF-003 Student is notified.
FR-REF-004 Employer sees educator referral provenance.
FR-REF-005 Lifecycle: referred -> viewed -> interview_requested -> interview_accepted/scheduled -> hired OR closed.
FR-REF-006 Institution sees referral velocity and status.

8.6 EMPLOYER VERIFICATION / HIRING PROFILE

FR-EMP-001 Collect legal/display name, contact, service area, trades, hiring volume, role types, shift/work needs, driver requirements, screening expectations, target hire timing.
FR-EMP-002 Employer remains pending until TXKPRO verification.
FR-EMP-003 Employer users receive organization-scoped access.
FR-EMP-004 Store plan/entitlement field for Core/Growth/Enterprise.
FR-EMP-005 No in-platform subscription payment required in MVP1.

8.7 TALENT DISCOVERY / JOB-READY FILTER

FR-MAT-001 Approved employers can browse discoverable students in authorized programs/cohorts.
FR-MAT-002 Filters:
- program;
- graduation timing;
- verified skill(s);
- minimum verified skills;
- driver’s license status;
- driving-record attestation;
- screening willingness;
- work type;
- shift;
- location;
- educator referral status.
FR-MAT-003 Show provenance of each filterable signal.
FR-MAT-004 Results are not final hiring recommendations.
FR-MAT-005 Employers can save candidate shortlist.

MVP1 decision:
Use deterministic filters only.

8.8 INTERVIEW REQUESTS

FR-INT-001 Employer can request interview from candidate profile.
FR-INT-002 Store employer, student, role/trade, message, optional scheduling link, timestamp, status.
FR-INT-003 Send SMS when student has phone + SMS consent.
FR-INT-004 Email/in-app fallback.
FR-INT-005 Student can accept, decline, or request follow-up.
FR-INT-006 Authorized dashboards show outcome.

8.9 PLACEMENT / HIRE

FR-PLC-001 Employer can mark candidate hired.
FR-PLC-002 Store student, employer, role/trade, hire date, employment type, source referral/match, status.
FR-PLC-003 Hire triggers retention milestones.
FR-PLC-004 Institution sees aggregate outcomes.
FR-PLC-005 Employment-state changes are audited.
FR-PLC-006 D-10: Record hire creates pending_start regardless of scheduled date. Explicit authorized actual-start confirmation is required for official placement counts. Retention responses do not change placement status.

8.10 30/60/90-DAY RETENTION PULSE

FR-RET-001 Hire creates Day 30, 60, 90 milestones.
FR-RET-002 Send student SMS if phone + consent exist.
FR-RET-003 Simple scale: 1=Going well, 2=Okay/some friction, 3=Need help.
FR-RET-004 “3” creates intervention case.
FR-RET-005 Authorized institution/TXKPRO users see case.
FR-RET-006 Case supports owner, notes, status, resolution.
FR-RET-007 Dashboard shows milestone completion/risk.
FR-RET-008 No automated employment action from response.

MVP1 decision:
Owner-approved D-04 decision (2026-10-04, GitHub #216): MVP1 includes Student-only retention pulses. Employer-side pulses are deferred to a separately approved pilot/backlog task. Student pulse is the minimum source-backed requirement. This decision adds no Employer access to Student responses or internal case notes and no automatic employment consequence. Future Employer pulse work requires an explicit consent, data ownership, role/scope, event and delivery contract before implementation.

17. RISKS

R1 Low employer liquidity
Mitigation: 5-10 committed employers in one concentrated pilot.

R2 Instructor workload
Mitigation: one-click verification aligned to syllabus.

R3 Ambiguous “clean driving record”
Mitigation: provenance labels; employer performs final eligibility verification.

R4 Gamification distorts employability
Mitigation: engagement never changes verified skill; defer full points economy.

R5 Data overcollection
Mitigation: minimum workforce data only.

R6 SMS consent/deliverability
Mitigation: consent timestamps, opt-out handling, logs, fallback.

R7 Unverified external statistics
Mitigation: validate before publication.
