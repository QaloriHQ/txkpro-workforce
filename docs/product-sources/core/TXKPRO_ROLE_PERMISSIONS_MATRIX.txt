TXKPRO™ WORKFORCE
SHARED ROLE & PERMISSIONS MATRIX
Version 1.0 — September 2026

PURPOSE
Define the canonical Workforce role vocabulary, role intent, scope model, and cross-app permission expectations. This file is a product/architecture source of truth. Prototype "View as" controls are demonstration-only and are not production authorization.

CANONICAL ROLE FAMILIES

PLATFORM
- super_admin: unrestricted TXKPRO platform administration.
- admin: platform operations with broad but policy-limited authority.
- support: customer support/operations access only where explicitly granted.
- read_only_analyst: aggregate/reporting access where assigned.

INSTITUTION
- institution_admin: institution-wide workforce administration.
- department_head: department/program-group management and reporting.
- program_coordinator: program/cohort operations, roster, referral, placement coordination.
- instructor: assigned student/cohort skill verification and referral workflow.
- assistant_instructor: assigned cohort support; may prepare observations/review items but authoritative verification can require instructor approval.
- career_services: readiness, employer relationship, referral, interview, placement, retention operations.

EMPLOYER
- employer_owner: employer account ownership, team, hiring configuration, candidate access.
- employer_admin: employer operational administration.
- recruiter: candidate discovery, referrals, interviews, hiring pipeline.
- hiring_manager: scoped candidate and interview/hire workflows.
- employer_read_only: permitted candidate/pipeline reporting without mutation.
- employer_employee: own training resources and explicitly accepted incentive programs only; no candidate, hiring, team administration, course authoring or tenant reporting. Owner-approved staging expansion 2026-10-05 (#76).

STUDENT
- student: owns student profile, preferences, self-attestations, responses, visibility settings, and consent-controlled communications.

SCOPE TYPES
- platform
- institution
- department
- program
- cohort
- employer
- self

AUTHORIZATION PRINCIPLES
1. Authentication proves identity; it does not imply application authority.
2. Production permissions are evaluated server-side.
3. Role + scope are both required.
4. User-editable profile metadata is never authorization proof.
5. A person may hold multiple memberships.
6. Cross-tenant access is denied by default.
7. Every sensitive mutation is auditable.
8. Prototype role toggles must never be reused as production auth logic.

CROSS-APP VISIBILITY PRINCIPLES
- Student-controlled visibility gates employer discovery.
- Institution users see only students within authorized educational/workforce scope.
- Employers see only approved/discoverable candidate data and their own hiring activity.
- Institution users do not see employers' private HR notes/ratings.
- Employers do not see institution-only internal notes.
- Students do not see restricted institution/admin audit details.
- Platform admins access only what operational policy authorizes; high-impact actions are logged.

D-10 APPROVED CLARIFICATION — 2026-10-03
Official placement requires an authorized Employer or permitted workforce operator confirming the Student started employment. Scheduled starts remain pending_start; retention milestones remain separate. Recording a hire alone does not establish started employment. Historical and scoped official counts require explicit confirmation evidence. Existing active/ended status values and PLACEMENT_CREATED / PLACEMENT_STATUS_CHANGED audit vocabulary remain; no retention status is a placement status. Role, evidence, reporting, legacy-history and verification details: docs/D10_OFFICIAL_PLACEMENT.md (#202).

AuthCard sandbox (#257): active users manage only their own private details, named request sharing and per-order authorization. Existing explicit workspace screening grants/scope govern requests and submissions. Shared KYU is not authorization or employment eligibility. Full contract: docs/governance/AUTHCARD_STAGING.md.

W12-20 COMMUNITY PERMISSIONS — OWNER APPROVED 2026-10-12 (#59)
Any active natural-person Workforce account with an active role membership may publish a public post/article/update and may like, comment/reply, share or repost published public content. Advertiser-only and inactive accounts are denied. Actor identity is server-derived; no role selector or user metadata grants access.
Authors manage only their own profile posts through the existing owner RPC. Published, hidden and removed moderation states are preserved. Public activity-history preferences are owner-only and control the full history plus like/comment/share/repost categories independently without deleting canonical records. Anonymous visitors do not receive community mutation access, and no social permission grants tenant, candidate, institution, Employer-private or other Workforce-record access.

W12-18 / W12-19 / W12-21 PUBLIC TRUST — OWNER APPROVED 2026-10-13 (#57, #58, #60)
Employer public-profile management: employer_owner and employer_admin in the matching approved, active Employer scope.
Institution public-profile management: institution_super_admin and institution_admin in the matching active Institution scope.
Educator review eligibility: active Students; other active Educators; and Institution leadership only when its active scope matches the Educator's current Institution. Self-review is denied. Client-selected role labels never grant access.
Review moderation: TXKPRO platform administration only. Educator profile owners may hide public review/rating presentation but may not publish, edit or remove another user's review.

ADS-01 NATIVE DELIVERY PERMISSIONS — OWNER APPROVED 2026-10-16 (#265, #275)
- Public and signed-in visitors may receive only eligible, approved sponsored presentation through the server selector. They receive no direct advertising-table access.
- Signed-in active Workforce members may report a served ad. The server derives reporter identity and preserves a bounded creative snapshot as restricted moderation evidence.
- Viewable/clicked measurement and ad selection are service-role-only functions; request parameters never grant authority or access to campaign records.
- Platform administrators may open the presentation-only four-placement preview. Previewing does not serve inventory, spend budget or activate an external provider.
- Advertiser self-service, campaign/creative review and funding controls are deferred to ADS-02. No Student, Institution or Employer role receives those permissions in ADS-01.

ADS-02 ADVERTISER WORKSPACE PERMISSIONS — OWNER APPROVED 2026-10-16 (#264, #276)
- Advertiser Owner: full advertiser-scoped team, campaign, creative, Stripe sandbox funding, submission, pause/resume, credit/allocation history and aggregate analytics access.
- Advertiser Admin: advertiser-scoped team administration, Stripe sandbox funding and full campaign operations except transferring/removing the Owner.
- Advertiser Creative: advertiser-scoped campaign and creative draft editing plus previews; no team, approval, funding or lifecycle-finalization authority.
- Advertiser Analyst: advertiser-scoped read-only campaign, allocation-versus-spend and aggregate delivery analytics.
- TXKPRO platform Admin: advertiser suspension/reactivation and campaign/creative review decisions. Admin does not approve self-service advertiser registration or manually create campaign allocation; approval fails closed unless the advertiser has already funded and reserved the full campaign budget.
- Advertiser-only membership is stored outside app_role_memberships. It grants no Student, Institution, Employer, private Workforce, community-authoring or cross-advertiser permission.

ADS-03 ANALYTICS AND RECONCILIATION PERMISSIONS — OWNER APPROVED 2026-10-16 (#265, #277)
- Advertiser Owner, Admin, Creative and Analyst may read/export only aggregate analytics for their advertiser scope. Exports are audited.
- TXKPRO platform Admin may append invalid-traffic and provider-reconciliation evidence. Advertisers cannot invalidate delivery or record spend.
- No role receives viewer identities, private Workforce data or direct advertising-table access through analytics.

ADS-04 (#278): public/signed-in visitors may receive MGID fallback only in approved public contexts. External delivery tables are service-role-only with RLS and revoked anon/authenticated table access. Random per-delivery tokens authorize only bounded, idempotent measurement. Reporting additionally requires an active canonical community actor; no advertiser receives private viewer data. Only platform-scoped Super Admin may inspect MGID integration configuration/aggregate delivery status. Contract: docs/integrations/mgid-json-native-feed.md.

EMPLOYER INCENTIVE EXECUTION — #368
Approved active Employer Owner/Admin: configure immutable draft execution before
acceptance, scoped verified event/review/employment, delegation/revocation,
integration mapping/binding/reconciliation, tier/perk fulfillment and funding.
Active accepted participant: own giving allowance, own bank redemption, own
individual exception (explicit assigned crew lead for crew exception), own perk
request and recognition consent. Program-specific safety_reviewer may perform
only the independent safety stage while participation remains active. A safety
reviewer may not approve their own reward or both safety and manager stages.
Natural-person employment verification and peer approvals are independent.
Machine integration credentials authorize only their program/bound employees and
configured event types; they never create a human staff role. Worker job RPCs
are service-only. Direct table mutation and anonymous reads remain denied.

EMPLOYER CREWS — #368
Approved active Employer Owner/Admin: create reusable crews and select active
members from their own Employer directory. Recruiter, Hiring Manager and
Employer Read-only: view Employer-scoped crews only. Employer Employee: no
operational crew-directory access. Selecting or joining a crew never grants a
role, program participation, hiring access, verified skill or reward entitlement.

ATS-03 APPLICATION PERMISSIONS — OWNER APPROVED 2026-11-01 (#330)
- Employer Owner/Admin: invite candidates and read/manage all applications and tasks in their active approved Employer scope.
- Recruiter: invite candidates and read/manage applications and tasks in their Employer scope; cannot publish Pipeline Versions or Job Listings through ATS-03.
- Hiring Manager: invite, read and manage only applications for explicitly assigned Jobs.
- Employer Read-only: read Employer-scoped applications and task status; cannot invite, withdraw, close, move or override.
- Student applicant: submit one application per Job from their verified identity; read/withdraw only their own application and read/submit only candidate-visible owned tasks.
- External applicant: verified-email limited session with the same own-application boundary; no Student, Institution or Employer membership is created.
- Institution and unrelated Employer actors: denied. Provider completion and any task transition never create an automatic accept, reject, hire or confirmed employment start.
